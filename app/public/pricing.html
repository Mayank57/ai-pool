Billing questions

What counts as a connected plan?

Each Claude or ChatGPT subscription you sign in to the pool. Overflow API keys are free to connect; their usage is billed by the provider per token.

What happens when the trial ends?

Requests through your keys return a "trial ended" error until the workspace owner activates billing. Your plans, keys and history stay as they are.

Do members pay anything?

No. Members join free and can optionally connect their own plan.
